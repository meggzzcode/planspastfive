Tracking is ON (`planspastfive-20`); change nothing.

**Why:** the picnic article should recommend things its reader would be genuinely excited to own or give, not utilities. Our audience: women who want a beautiful, easy afternoon outside; she loves vintage, botanical, gingham, linen and warm natural looks, but not everything pink. Shop rule: sold by Amazon.com or the brand (never a third-party seller), exact variant, contents as listed, and good-looking.

Push to `cowork/batch-6` only: `docs/cowork/BATCH-6-NOTES.md` and `docs/cowork/BATCH-6-RESULTS.csv` (columns as in `BATCH-5-RESULTS.csv`, plus `look_in_your_words` and `would_she_want_it`). Message the owner a short summary leading with anything not OK.

In `look_in_your_words` describe what you can actually see in the listing images (colours, pattern, material, finish) in your own words, not Amazon's automatic descriptions. In `would_she_want_it` give one honest sentence from the point of view of our reader: would she be excited to own or give this, or does it look generic or cheap?
1. **A picnic basket set for two (and one for four):** a willow or wicker basket with a lid, with plates, cutlery and napkins (glasses if included). List the pieces and their materials; say if the plates are real melamine, enamel or plastic; is the basket lined; does it have a closure; sold by Amazon or the brand.
2. **A good-looking picnic blanket:** gingham, floral, linen-look or a warm natural print; large enough for 2 to 4 sitting; waterproof backing stated; **machine washable stated or not**; folds into a carry strap. Sold by Amazon or the brand.
3. **A travel cheese or charcuterie board:** wood or bamboo board that carries (lid, bag or case), with knives or a small tool set. Sold by Amazon or the brand.
4. **Shatterproof drinks:** stemless acrylic or stainless tumblers or wine glasses in a set of 2 to 4 that look good (not obviously plastic); note insulated or not.
5. **Enamel or vintage-look plates and mugs** for two to four, as an alternative to the basket set's own plates.
6. **A small flower or herb jar or bud vase** that is safe to carry (a lidded glass jar or a weighted vase), only if something sold by Amazon or the brand looks genuinely lovely and travels.
7. One more idea of your own that fits a beautiful afternoon outside (a quilted tote, a linen napkin set, a picnic game such as a travel card set). Explain why.
