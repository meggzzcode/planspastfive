## What we will NOT do (stated in every request)
No editing, cropping, recolouring or retouching of the product or its image (proportional resizing only). No implying endorsement or sponsorship. No use of the image anywhere except beside our own recommendation of that product. No claim that we tested the product.

## The questions every request asks
(a) Is the use permitted, and under what conditions (credit wording, no alteration, approval before use)? (b) Does permission cover a commercial website that earns affiliate commission? (c) **Can the permission continue if the website is sold to a new owner (assignable or transferable), or must the buyer apply again?** (d) Is there a media library or press kit we should use, or will you supply a file? (e) Please confirm your answer in writing (a reply email is enough).

## When a reply arrives: what to record
1. Save the full reply (text, sender, date) in the owner's records folder; do not paraphrase it.
2. Write down: permission text (exact words); date; **scope** (which images, which pages, commercial or affiliate stated yes/no); **transferability** (yes / no / not stated: if not stated, treat as no and tell any buyer); attribution wording required; any approval or expiry condition.
3. If permission is given and the image file is supplied, add the file to `public/images/` and a record in `src/content/images.json` (see `docs/OWNER-OPERATIONS.md`): `kind` product-photo, `credit` (the brand), `source` (name and the media library or email reference), `licenceName` (e.g. "Written permission, <brand>, <date>"), `licenceCheckedOn`, `commercialUse` yes only if the reply says so, `transferWithSite` yes / no / unverified exactly as the reply, `attribution` (their wording or none), `approvedOn`, and `rightsStatus: approved` only when every required field is filled. Otherwise leave it `pending`.
4. No reply, or a refusal, or no commercial permission: the card stays text-only. Record the date and do not use the image.
5. Tell the AI or developer when a reply arrives so the record is checked against the build.
