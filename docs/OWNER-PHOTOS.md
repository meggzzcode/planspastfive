# Owner photography (short guide)

**You do not need to photograph every product, and owner photography is never a launch requirement.** A product card with no picture stays text-only (name, the maker's own claims, the Amazon link, the disclosure). That is a finished card, not a gap.

## Photograph these 2 to 4 (in this order)
1. **Tombow MONO Permanent applicator.** It is the hero product of the Stage 2 junk-journal article and the demo article, and Tombow publishes no image library or usage terms, so an owner-original photo is the fastest clean route.
2. **The 2026 mahjong card, cover or box only.** The card's contents are copyrighted, so a permission request is slow and may fail; a photo of the closed exterior is fine for you to take. Never an open page or any hand. This is the hero product of the mahjong article.
3. **Any item whose packaging is part of its appeal** that you already own (a journal, a washi tape, a sticker set): photograph it because it is already at hand and costs nothing.
4. **One more only if you already own it** and its brand has no media programme (most brands in the photo notes list no press route).

## Skip
Anything you would have to buy just to photograph; products from brands that reply to the permission request with a usable file; scissors, pens and generic supplies (a text card is fine); the on-hold Rifle set. Do not use Amazon's pictures, and do not use stock photos as product pictures. If a brand grants permission, you do not need to photograph that product.

## Shot guide (6 lines)
1. **Light:** daylight beside a window, no flash; a white card opposite as a reflector.
2. **Background:** plain white or kraft paper, nothing patterned.
3. **Framing:** the product straight on, filling about two-thirds of the frame, label readable.
4. **One product only:** nothing else in the picture.
5. **No other brands:** remove other logos, packaging and screens from the frame.
6. **Crop square, 1600 x 1600 px**, JPG. Name it `<product-id>-hero.jpg` (for example `tombow-mono-permanent-hero.jpg`).

## How to send them
Attach the files in the chat with the AI and say, for each one: the product, the date you took it, and "I took this photo". The AI will add the file and its rights record (`kind` product-photo, credit "Photo: Plans Past Five", `commercialUse` yes, `transferWithSite` yes, since you own the copyright; you keep the original files and hand them over with the site if you sell it). Caption it only as "our photo of the product we bought"; do not claim testing.
