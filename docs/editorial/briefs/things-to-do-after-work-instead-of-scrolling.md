## Research Brief

**Search intent.** Mostly informational and "I want ideas now": a woman with a 9 to 5 who wants a better evening than her phone, with low energy and little time. Secondary intent: how to actually stop reaching for the phone.

**Long-tail questions to serve** (no volume data): what to do after work instead of scrolling; things to do after work alone; low-effort things to do at home after work; how to stop doomscrolling in the evening; what to do when you are too tired to do anything; 10-minute evening activities; how to build an evening routine without a hobby; how to make a library card; screen-free evening ideas for adults.

**Gap found.** Typical lists in this space (as seen in search snippets and the NPR/Common Sense phone-habit titles; the NPR pages themselves returned 503, so not read) are either hobby lists, or "put your phone down" advice with no replacement plan. Unanswered: what to do with exactly 10 minutes; how to make the first step small; how to set up the evening before you are tired. This article sorts by time and puts the switch mechanics (move the phone, decide earlier, set out, default) up front.
