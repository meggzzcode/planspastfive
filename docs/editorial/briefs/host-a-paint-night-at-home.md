## Research Brief

**Search intent.** Informational and planning: "how to host a paint night at home", "paint and sip at home", "paint party for adults at home". Long-tail questions found in the top guides and answered here: how many people fit at a table; what supplies each person needs; how long a paint night lasts; how to protect the table; what to do if guests say they can't paint; how to keep snacks and drinks off the paint; what non-drinkers get; how to clean up; what to do with wet paintings; what to write in the invitation; what if it falls through.

**What top guides do and miss.** The commercial guides (Muse Paintbar, Art Shed, Painting to Gogh, Step by Step Painting) are mostly supply lists and party themes, and several sell kits. Gaps this article fills:
- A clock-time schedule with an honest total (2.5 hours) that matches our hobbies guide.
- Counting painters, not guests, and the kit arithmetic (host paints too).
- A decision between guided (same project) and free choice, with a recommendation.
- Real inclusion of non-drinkers (the guides mention mocktails in one line).
- Cleanup grounded in an acrylic maker's guidance (Liquitex) and a state health department, rather than "rinse it".
- A rain-check plan and a copy-ready invitation (none of the guides had either).
- Plain "do not promise a finishing time" honesty about kit timing.

**Key sourced facts used.** Template or guide lowers nerves (Yahoo, Painting to Gogh, Muse); paint a sample first (Muse, Step by Step, Art Shed); 5 x 3 ft dining table fits about six easels (Step by Step); start before 7 p.m., about two hours of painting is tiring (Step by Step); tutorials 1 to 2 hours plus about 30 minutes for pauses (Painting to Gogh); paint first, snack while canvases dry (Yahoo); drop cloths, disposable cloths or kraft paper (Muse, Art Shed, Yahoo, Painting to Gogh); lamps/window light (Muse); ribbon on water jars (Art Shed); non-staining snacks (Muse); alcohol-free options, more effort on food, label alcohol, no explaining (NIAAA); wash hands before eating, swirl brush in water not mouth, wipe tools before washing, dedicated waste container, do not wash paint sludge down the sink (Liquitex); do not pour hazardous art wastewater down the drain, ask local health department if unsure (WA DOH); order kits a week ahead (Painting to Gogh).
