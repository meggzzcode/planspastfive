**Verified activity facts (each with source)**
- Perishable food: no more than 2 hours in the 40-140F danger zone, 1 hour above 90F; cold at 40F or below; hot at 140F or above; fill the cooler, use ice or gel packs, separate drinks cooler, keep closed. FDA, Handling Food Safely While Eating Outdoors (read in full).
- Hands washed 20 seconds; water jug, soap and paper towels or moist towelettes if no tap; wash produce at home. FDA (same page).
- Pack small amounts to reduce leftovers; throw away perishable leftovers before heading home; wash cooler afterward; choose cold dishes unless hot can be held at 140F+. Penn State Extension, Healthy and Safe Picnics (read in full).
- Park rules differ by place: Raleigh Lake Johnson shelter page says no alcohol of any kind, personal grills at least 15 ft from shelters and trees, candles/sparklers banned, shelters first come unless reserved, all trash in bins. Fairfax County page: alcohol only with permits and approved agreements, open fire banned at events, shelter reservations not refundable for weather, balloons and taped decorations not allowed. Both read in full.
- Menu: sturdy foods that hold at room temperature; avoid soggy or fragile (ice cream, layer cake, juicy pie); pack delicate greens and dressing separately; mayo-free options; check whether glass is allowed. Food Network, How to Plan the Perfect Picnic (read in full).
- Make-ahead: hearty salads dressed ahead, hard-boiled eggs 1 to 2 days ahead, herbs separate, wraps rolled tightly in foil, brownies better on day two. Love and Lemons, 51 Picnic Food Ideas (read in full). Note: I did not repeat their deviled egg advice in the menu because Food Network lists deviled eggs as a food to avoid and no source read confirms transport safety.
- UV strongest 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.; shade, cover arms and legs, wide-brimmed hat, wraparound sunglasses, broad-spectrum SPF 15+. CDC skin cancer prevention page (read in full). The page does not say "reapply every 2 hours", so that is left out.
- Repellent: EPA-registered products (DEET, picaridin, IR3535, OLE, PMD, 2-undecanone); sunscreen first, repellent second; loose long sleeves. CDC mosquito prevention page (read in full).
- Leave No Trace: plan ahead (learn rules, keep groups small, repackage food), pack out all trash and leftover food, never feed animals. National Park Service, Leave No Trace Seven Principles (read in full). These are written for hikers and campers; the article says so and applies three of them.

**Beginner requirements:** a blanket, bag, cooler or insulated bag with ice packs, wipes or soap and water, trash bag, cutlery, sun and bug protection, one rain plan.

**Obstacles:** food safety anxiety; heat; unknown park rules (alcohol, grills, glass); weather; carrying everything; deciding what to cook.
